## 🎥 Recommended 3–5 Minute Gameplay Video Recording Plan

1. **0:00 – 0:30 | Title & Menus:**
   - Display the main menu: title, subtitle, and Victorian parchment aesthetic.
   - Click "How Not to Explode" (Operator's Manual) and "Questionable Settings".
   - Show the "Ministry Records" leaderboard connecting to the backend API.

2. **0:30 – 1:30 | Core Mechanics & The Bureaucratic Jump:**
   - Click "Commence Operation" to begin.
   - Demonstrate clumsy steam momentum [A / D].
   - Press [Space] with < 20% pressure to show the comedic sad wheeze and dust puff.
   - Walk to pump boiler pressure above 35%, then perform the solid steam-powered jump.
   - Show Sir Clanksworth giving unhelpful advice in his brass telegraph speech bubble.

3. **1:30 – 2:30 | Signature Terrible Mechanics:**
   - Walk into the Gearbox Inversion Zone: showcase the loud clunk and *"LEFT NOW MEANS EMOTIONAL RIGHT"* prompt as controls swap.
   - Let pressure build to 100%: demonstrate the flashing red overheat alarm, shaking boiler, screeching steam whistles, and hold [W] to vent steam and save the machine.
   - Approach the Patent Upgrade Terminal: press [E] and install **Gold-Plated Knees** or **Artisanal Steam Extract** to show the comical heavier stomps and golden sparkle exhaust.

4. **2:30 – 3:30 | Triplicate Door & Win Condition:**
   - Platform across the moving hydraulic piston elevator and hazard cogs.
   - Reach the Bureaucratic Triplicate Door: press [E] three times to stamp Forms 104-A, 104-B, and 104-C.
   - Deliver the brass gear into the Royal Office socket.
   - Show the victory fanfare, confetti steam fireworks, and official Royal Certificate of Completion with stats and engineer rank!

5. **3:30 – 4:00 | Comedic Failure (Optional / Bonus):**
   - Press [R] to restart quickly, walk into a hazard cog or allow boiler to detonate.
   - Show the explosion shrapnel, funny cause of death banner, and Sir Clanksworth's eulogy.
